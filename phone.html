Aim at something printed — a page, a book, a mat. Freeze, drag the four corners onto it, place. The stage then appears here, over your camera.

Placing the stage…
  • Prop the phone up facing you, roughly where a webcam would be.
  • Keep this page open — leaving it stops the camera.
  • Both devices have to be on the same Wi-Fi.
